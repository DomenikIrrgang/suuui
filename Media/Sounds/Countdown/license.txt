SuuUI's countdown voices ("one" to "ten" and "pull"), generated for SuuUI with
tools/gen_countdown_voice.py from Piper text-to-speech voice models (VITS),
trimmed, normalised and encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

Female: Piper voice en_US-ljspeech-high
  Trained from scratch on the LJ Speech dataset (public domain),
  https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/
  Model by Bryce Beattie, https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/

Male: Piper voice en_US-norman-medium
  Trained from scratch on recordings from LibriVox (public domain),
  https://librivox.org
  Model by Bryce Beattie, https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/

Models: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices
