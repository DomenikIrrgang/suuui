SuuUI's countdown voices ("one" to "ten" and "pull"), generated for SuuUI with
tools/gen_countdown_voice.py from Kokoro-82M, an open-weight text-to-speech
model by hexgrad released under the Apache License 2.0
(https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), using its voices af_heart
(Heart), af_bella (Bella), am_michael (Michael), am_fenrir (Fenrir) and
am_puck (Puck); run through the kokoro-onnx export
(https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx).

The generated recordings are released with SuuUI under the MIT License.
